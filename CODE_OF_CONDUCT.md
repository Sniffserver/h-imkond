# HÕIMU Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the spirit of bioregional mutual aid, kinship (*Hõim*), and ecological stewardship, we pledge to foster an inclusive, welcoming, and safe community for everyone regardless of background, identity, or technical experience.

## Community Standards

- **Mutual Respect:** Practice patience, active listening, and constructive collaboration.
- **Privacy & Autonomy:** Honor pseudonymous identities and data sovereignty.
- **Inclusivity:** Welcome newcomers, permaculturists, radio enthusiasts, and coders alike.
